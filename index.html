Good questions

        
Before you book

      

      

        

          
What do I need to have ready?
A flat, safe spot with room to open the doors and hood, and the keys. Apartment lots and workplace lots are usually fine. If your HOA or building has rules about repairs, let us know in the chat.

          
Do you bring the parts?
Yes. Once the quote is agreed, the right parts are picked up before the visit. Want to supply your own? That's fine; the labor price stays the same and the warranty covers the work only.

          
Is the work guaranteed?
Parts and labor are covered by a 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty, whichever comes first. If something isn't right, message on WhatsApp and it gets fixed.

        

        

          
Can you work on my Tesla, Leaf or Prius?
Yes for maintenance and the common faults: 12V batteries, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension, tire rotation, charging diagnosis and hybrid battery health tests. Repairs inside the high-voltage battery pack need a specialist shop, and we'll tell you honestly if that's what your car needs.

          
How do I pay?
After the job is done and you've checked it. Card, Zelle, Apple Pay, Cash App or cash.

          
Is there a trip fee?
Not inside the 30-mile radius when you book a repair. A diagnostic-only visit is $59, credited toward the repair if you go ahead.
